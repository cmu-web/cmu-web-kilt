Pathways

Sections that send people on to their next step: path cards, an I Want To list, link banks, a coming-soon card and an FAQ teaser.

Path Cards CMS

Whole-card links that send each audience to its own page. They fit as many columns as the width allows; cmu-paths--two keeps a pair side by side. The call-to-action line (cta) is the template's own. For a page with just two main destinations, use Big Buttons.

I Want To… Template

The same grid without a color: white boxes with a thin border on the page's own background, headed by what people come to do. Here each box's title is the link, in the template's red, instead of a call to action (cta) under the text. Use linked titles when the title says it all, and a call to action when the action deserves its own words. Phrase the titles as the end of the heading's sentence: "I want to… Borrow a Camera."

Link Bank Template

Red columns of links at the bottom of a page, to the site's main pages and its neighbors. It's a grid with nav, and each column a nav-list; darkgrey invert works too. Keep it to links: a way into an app goes in the page, not here. A column can start with an <h1>, which shows as a small bold label.

On This Page Link Bank CMS

Links to the sections of a long page, in columns under its intro, for a page whose content uses the full width, such as wide tables or examples. It's a white link bank with cmu-on-this-page and a cmu-label first: the label takes a row of its own over the columns, and the link bank drops the template's top and bottom padding, which only gives a section with a background room. The links stay the page's usual red, so they read as links.

  • Not on a page with a sidebar: there, the On This Page box goes at the top of the sidebar.
  • Not on a page short enough to see at a glance.

Question Mark / Coming Soon Card CMS

A card for something not decided yet: an open spot, a speaker to be announced or a call for suggestions. This is a Card Grid card, with one of PlaceMellon's Scotty question marks as its picture:

  • ?scotty=recumbent&question: Scotty in a play bow beside the question mark.
  • ?scotty=rampant&question: Scotty standing up as the question mark's dot.

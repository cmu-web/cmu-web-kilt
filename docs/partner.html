Get Help With Your Project

We help with web apps used across CMU and with giving hosted services (SaaS) a CMU look. Tell us what you're working on and where you need help.

Fit Your App to CMU's Template

We can help you choose a page layout, work through navigation and forms, and use the kit's components in your app. Bring an existing app, a prototype or an idea you're still working out.

For a hosted service, such as a form, survey or help-desk tool, tell us which parts of its pages you can change, and we'll help you apply CMU's styles within those limits.

To get started, fill out the request for help [login required]. Your answers aren't public, so private links and screenshots are fine.

Ask for a Design Review

Already built? We can check your app's pages against CMU's template and the kit's conventions and send you a list of what to change, most important first. Fill out the design review request [login required]. Your answers stay private.

Building a Website?

For a department, program or event website, talk to University Communications and Marketing. Their Contact Us page has a project request form.

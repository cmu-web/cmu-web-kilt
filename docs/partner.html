Partner With Us

Building an app for people across CMU, or giving an existing service a CMU look? Tell us what you're working on and where you need help.

Fit Your App to CMU's Template

We can help you choose a page layout, work through navigation and forms, and use the kit's components in your app. Bring an existing service, a prototype or an idea you're still working out.

Tell Us About Your Project

Include a few details so we can understand what you need:

  • The app's name and the office or team building it.
  • Who will use it and what they'll need to do.
  • Which screens or workflows you want help with.
  • Any target dates or constraints.
